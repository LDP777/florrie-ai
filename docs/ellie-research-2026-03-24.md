# Ellie — User Research Interview
**Date:** 24 March 2026
**Interviewer:** Florrie AI (Claude PA)
**Subject:** Ellie — Levi's partner, solo beautician, primary test user

## Profile
- **Treatments:** Brows (lamination, wax, tint) + Lashes (extensions, lifts)
- **Scale:** 50–100 active clients, 20–30 appointments/week
- **Current system:** Timely (paid monthly salon management)
- **Location:** UK

## Morning Routine
- First thing: **checks DMs/messages** (Instagram, WhatsApp)
- Not diary-first — messages come first

## Pain Points (ranked by her)
1. **Social media content** — "all of it honestly" — ideas, captions, consistency, posting. The whole thing feels like a chore.
2. **Admin & money** — doesn't really track income or expenses. Figures it out at tax time. No spreadsheet, no accounting app.
3. Clients drifting — dormant clients **just drift away**. She doesn't chase them. Notices eventually but by then it feels awkward.

## What's NOT a pain
- **Booking flow is fast** — 2-3 messages and it's confirmed. Not a saga.
- **Client tracking** — Timely handles basic records. She's not scrambling for client info.

## Current Tools
- **Timely** — booking, client records, probably reminders
- **No money tracking** at all
- **No content system** — ad hoc posting when she can be bothered

## AI Trust Level
- **"Close enough is fine"** — doesn't need the AI to perfectly clone her voice
- Friendly and professional = good enough
- This is great news for the confidence-gated model — we can start more autonomous

## Magic Wand
- When asked to pick one: **"all of them"**
- Wants clients to never go quiet, social media to run itself, money always tracked, no more no-shows
- She's the dream customer — wants full automation, not just a tool

## Key Implications for Florrie

### Priority Reorder
Based on Ellie's actual pain, the build priority shifts:

1. **Content Autopilot** — her #1 pain. If Florrie can draft posts, suggest before/afters, and queue content, she'd feel the value immediately.
2. **Money Tracker** — her #2 pain. She literally doesn't track anything. Receipt scanning + auto-categorisation would be transformative for her tax situation.
3. **Comeback Engine** — clients drift and she doesn't chase. This is pure revenue recovery that she's currently leaving on the table.
4. **AI Front Desk** — still valuable (she checks DMs first thing) but her booking flow is already efficient at 2-3 messages. The AI saves time but doesn't solve a burning pain.

### Timely Integration Question
She's ON Timely. Florrie needs to either:
- Replace Timely entirely (big ask — she's comfortable there)
- Integrate with Timely (API? Import?)
- Position as "the AI layer on top" — Florrie handles what Timely can't (content, money, client intelligence, AI messaging)

### Tone Model Starting Point
- Doesn't need perfection → can start with a warm/friendly/casual UK beautician tone
- She won't be scrutinising every word → more autonomy from day 1
- Low barrier to trust = faster adoption
