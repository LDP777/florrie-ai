Product truth in one line: Florrie answers a beautician's clients on WhatsApp, SMS and email in her own voice, fills her diary, offers cancellations to the waitlist within minutes, nudges rebooks, spots quiet weeks and bank holidays before she does, and keeps patch-test records. £29/mo, 120 messages included then 7p each, 14-day trial.

## 1. One-Page Strategy

### Beachhead: UK lash technicians
Lash techs run their whole business through WhatsApp and Instagram DMs. Infills every 2 to 3 weeks mean constant rebooking chatter, and a cancelled 2-hour slot is the most expensive gap in beauty. They also sit in the densest creator community in the trade: lash techs follow lash techs, copy each other's tools, and post their setups. One visible win travels further here than anywhere else in beauty. Patch-test records are a real compliance headache for them too, and Florrie already handles those.

Expansion rule: stay lash-only until 20 design partners are live, then open to nails and brows. Saying no to a keen nail tech in month one is the plan working.

### The target
20 founding design partners live by day 60. Ellie (Ellindigo) is partner #1 and the proof source for all launch content.

### The single growth loop
1. Florrie's Content engine flags filmable moments as they happen: a cancellation filled from the waitlist, the bank-holiday card, a reply clients couldn't tell from hers.
2. The partner screen-records it (or Levi films it with her) and posts it, credited **"my front desk is run by @florrie.ai"**.
3. Her audience is mostly other lash techs. They DM Florrie. Some become partners. More partners, more moments, more posts.

One loop, run hard. No paid ads, no SEO, no PR until the loop is measured.

### Referral mechanic
**Give a month, get a month.** Any user who refers a beauty pro gets a month free, and so does the new signup. For founding partners (already on 6 months free) referrals stack onto the end. Track manually in a spreadsheet until volume forces a build.

### 30-day calendar (~10 hrs/week of Levi's time)

**Week 1: Open the front door (10 hrs)**
- Build a list of 100 UK lash techs from Instagram hashtags and Ellie's follow graph. Log handle, city, follower count, last post. (2 hrs)
- Send the waitlist activation email (Section 4). (1 hr)
- Send 40 DMs using Section 3 variants. Log every send. (3 hrs)
- Film 2 moments with Ellie: the cancellation fill and the voice reveal. (2 hrs)
- Post Reel (a), publish the LinkedIn post (Section 6). (2 hrs)

**Week 2: First partners in (10 hrs)**
- 50 DMs, prioritising story-replies since they convert warmest. (3 hrs)
- Run discovery calls from week 1 replies, 15 minutes each. (3 hrs)
- Onboard the first 3 partners. Voice setup done for them on the call. (3 hrs)
- Post Reel (b). (1 hr)

**Week 3: The loop starts feeding itself (10 hrs)**
- 50 DMs, now including warm intros from live partners. (3 hrs)
- First weekly feedback calls with live partners, capture at least one filmable moment per partner. (3 hrs)
- Onboard the next 3 to 4 partners. (3 hrs)
- Post Reel (c) plus the first partner-credited clip. (1 hr)

**Week 4: Measure, prune, push (10 hrs)**
- Review DM reply rates by variant, kill the weakest, double the best. (1 hr)
- Onboard to 8 to 10 partners live. (4 hrs)
- Switch on give-a-month-get-a-month with live partners. (1 hr)
- Feedback calls, capture moments. (2 hrs)
- LinkedIn progress post with real numbers, plan month 2. (2 hrs)

## 7. Metrics That Matter (First 60 Days)

| Metric | Weekly target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| DMs sent | 50 | Top of funnel. Below 50, everything downstream starves. |
| Discovery calls booked | 5 | The conversion truth-teller. Under 10% DM-to-call means the message is off, fix it before sending more. |
| Partners live (cumulative) | +2 to 3 per week, 20 by day 60 | The only target that counts. "Live" means voice set up and Florrie answering real clients. |
| Filmable moments captured | 3 | Fuel for the growth loop. Zero in a week means the loop is dead, not resting. |
| Waitlist size | +25 | Lagging proof the content loop reaches beyond DMs. Came from a post or referral? Log the source. |

Review every Friday, 20 minutes. One metric red two weeks running gets the next week's hours.

## The Fresha moment (added 10 June, hours after dissecting their launch)

On 19 May Fresha launched Client Connect, Team Connect and AI Concierge:
in-app messaging, Slack-style team channels, and a customisable AI front
desk that captures bookings when asked. 229 likes on the announcement.
This is free market education. Every beautician now knows AI front desks
exist; our job is to be the answer to "yes, but one that works for ME".

### The line
**Fresha answers questions. Florrie runs your business.**

### Three talking points (use everywhere)
1. Theirs replies when spoken to. Ours acts on her own: fills cancellations
   from the waitlist, chases overdue rebooks, blocks bank holidays, spots
   quiet weeks. The done-list proves it every evening.
2. Theirs is a configurable bot inside the Fresha app. Ours learns how YOU
   talk from your real replies, and we measure it (the percent of drafts
   sent without an edit).
3. Theirs deepens the client's relationship with Fresha (their app, their
   inbox, their marketplace, their new-client commission). Ours strengthens
   your own number, your own name, your own clients.

### Objection: "I already use Fresha"
Keep it if you like, Florrie is not a booking system swap. She is the
worker: your WhatsApp answered as you, your gaps filled, your regulars
nudged back. Plenty of techs run Florrie for the relationship and keep a
listing wherever they want. The moment she fills one cancellation, she has
paid for two months.
