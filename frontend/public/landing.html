Chases patch tests.

Before every appointment, Florrie checks your patch test records and flags anyone who needs a closer look. One tap asks her to book a test. Florrie never decides who passed; only you do.

Florrie finds the open time in your next seven days, works out which treatments fit each gap, and offers the slot to clients who are due a rebook or waiting on your waitlist.

Your built-in AI voice commander. Hold the petal and talk: ask what tomorrow looks like, what you earned this week or who's due a rebook. Tell her to move a booking, message a client or send a payment link, and she lines it up for your yes.

Deposits at booking, card payments and payment links, all landing in one place. Florrie logs your takings as you finish each appointment, keeps your tax year running, and fills in expenses from a photo of the receipt.
