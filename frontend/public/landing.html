Questions

The things everyone asks.

Will my clients know they're talking to an AI? +

Honestly, most can't tell, because Florrie writes the way you write. You decide how she introduces herself, and you can review every reply before it goes out or let her send instantly. Plenty of pros start on review mode and switch once they trust her.

What if she gets something wrong? +

She only books what your diary actually allows, and anything unusual gets flagged to you instead of guessed at. Every action she takes is logged in the "What Florrie did" feed, so nothing happens behind your back.

Do I need a new phone number? +

No. Florrie connects to WhatsApp Business on the number your clients already use. Texts and emails plug in alongside. Nothing changes on their side.

What counts as a message? +

A message Florrie sends on your behalf: a reply, a confirmation, a reminder, a nudge. 120 a month covers a typical solo diary comfortably. Past that it's 7p each, and you can see your usage anytime.

Is my client data safe? +

Yes. UK-built, GDPR compliant, and your client list is yours: export it or delete it whenever you like. We never sell data or use your clients for anything except running your business.
